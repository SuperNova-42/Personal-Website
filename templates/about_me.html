About Me

My recent work includes building a custom Learning Management Platform and a real-time market data analyzer, prioritizing scalable backend architecture and robust data pipelines. I am currently pursuing my bachelor's of science degree in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, focusing on engineering functional, useful software.

As for me, my main programming language is Python, which I feel most comfortable with, but I have knowledge and experience in C and Java as well. I am bilingual, with spanish being my second language and I have an eagerness to learn more languages. The coding projects that most interest me and that I have made in the past are ones that tackle a problem that I have faced before in my life, whether it be a task that I need done or an idea I have to make something better. I lean more towards web development and data analysis in my more recent projects, but I have no problem learning new ways to solve problems and employing them when applicable and appropriate.

At UCF, I have taken important courses such as Computer Science I, which taught me foundations in data structures and algorithms, and Security in Computing, which taught me various foundations in Cybersecurity such as how data is secured and sent. Along with these computer science classes, I am taking Writing for the Technical Professional which is teaching me key skills in technical writing such as understanding your audience and choosing professional formatting and rhetoric according to your audience.
