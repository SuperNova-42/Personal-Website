&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;The purpose of this project was to create a tailored resume and cover letter for a real-world internship.
This was meant to get me to think more about audience and about highlighting information that is beneficial for presenting my qualifications to recruiters and hiring managers.
For this project, my chosen internship was one for IDX Exchange as a Software Developer Intern. 

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;My cover letter is meant to be a persuasive argument where every paragraph proves that I am qualified and ready for the job that I applied for. 
I used my cover letter to expand on my strongest resume points, such as my student status, and the projects that best highlight skills that are valuable to IDX Exchange for this position. 

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;For revisions, I rewrote the introduction to better highlight my student status, emphasized what I would bring to the company instead of what I hope to learn, and moved my projects to later on in the cover letter. 
I focused on providing qualifications and proving myself instead of just stating what I wanted to get out of the internship.

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Overall, I realized that a cover letter is supposed to be a persuasive document, not just an informative one. 
Additionally, I realized that it should highlight the qualities on my resume that pertain to the job listing as well as delve deeper into what I would bring to the company as a whole. 
I had to view this from the lens of a prospective employer instead of an eager applicant.
